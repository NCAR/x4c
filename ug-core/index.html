Core Features

x4c attaches a .x accessor to xarray.Dataset and xarray.DataArray that understands CESM conventions — where the grid-cell areas live, how to find latitude and longitude on an unstructured grid, and how the vertical coordinate is spaced.

Every notebook below runs against the reduced sample case bundled with these docs, so they work from a fresh clone. See the sample case README for what was trimmed out of it.

  • Overview — the .x accessor and what it attaches

  • Annualization and seasonalization — .x.annualize(), including DJF wraparound

  • Spatial means and climate indices — area-weighted .x.gm, .x.nhm, .x.shm

  • Regridding — SE and POP grids onto regular lat/lon

  • Visualization — .x.plot(), projections, and styles

  • Analysis: EOFs, site extraction, saving
